# T-132 renders

The Party tab in Normal, Smooth and Larger at 1x and 2x (`SidePanelPictures` mode `party`).

- `a-you-selected-by-default`: the room's title on top, the meter and chart, then your picture and name over your panels from the tiles on. The default: your part plain.
- `b-member-selected-after-their-room`: Brindle's part tinted toward her colour (the area behind it, 10%): the default.
- Options:
  - `a2-option-yours-tinted-too`: your part tinted too (toward white);
  - `b2-option-cards-tinted`: her cards tinted (12%) instead of the area;
  - `a3-option-cards-tinted-yours-too`: cards, yours too.
- `c-member-selected-mid-fight`: Quillon's live panels.
- `k-member-damage-on-adds`: Brindle's Damage card on `adds`, from her room message (Araxxor with araxytes).
